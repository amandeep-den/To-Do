Categories are automatic. A Short lead flag means the deadline allows fewer days than the average completion time. The workload panel warns when too many open tasks land together.

How forecasts, categories, and workload alerts work
  • Forecasts blend 5,000 tasks from 2025 with 3–21 day leads. Treat them as guidance, not a guarantee.
  • Categories are assigned automatically from the task title.
  • Short lead appears when the deadline gives fewer days than the average completion time. The on-time probability drops to reflect the shorter allocation.
  • Workload compares open tasks with deadlines in the same 3-day window against a realistic parallel capacity. Four or more personal tasks in that window is a heavy load.
  • Focus order ranks same-day collisions Urgent → High → Medium → Low, then the lowest on-time odds.
